# Assignment 3 — Box Net Technical Drawing in Onshape

## What was the goal?

The goal was to create a dimensioned 2D technical drawing of a box net in Onshape, following a given reference drawing. The sketch had to include the main body, the side flaps and the top and bottom lid details, use the exact lengths and angles from the reference, and be fully defined (all lines black).

## What did I do?

I created a new Onshape document named "Selen Koro – Kutu Açınımı" and drew the whole box net inside a single sketch on the Top plane.

- I drew the vertical centre axis as a construction line.
- I built the main body from 80 × 40 panels stacked along the axis.
- I added the 40-unit side panels, the 4-unit fold strips and the 38 × 36 end flaps with their 75° angled edges.
- I added the 20-unit dust flaps with 80° angled edges on the upper and lower panels.
- I drew the top tuck lid (14 units high) with its 130° corner chamfers and the 8, 4 and 2 unit details.
- I applied dimensions and geometric constraints until the sketch was fully defined.

## What tools and materials did I use?

I used Onshape in the browser. Inside the sketch I used the Line, Rectangle and Construction line tools, the Dimension tool for lengths and angles, and constraints such as Coincident, Horizontal, Vertical, Symmetric and Equal.

The project required a computer and an internet connection. No physical materials were used.

## What went wrong?

I missed the class where Onshape was introduced, so at first I did not know where to start or how a sketch becomes fully defined. Some of the small details in the reference drawing, such as the 4 / 2 / 40° details on the top lid and the 10° slopes, were also hard to read.

## What did I change? How did I solve the problem?

I broke the drawing down into parts (main body, side flaps, dust flaps, top lid) and drew them one at a time. I zoomed into the reference drawing to read the small dimensions and used Symmetric and Equal constraints so the left and right sides did not need separate dimensions.

## What was the result?

I completed a 2D technical drawing of the box net in Onshape based on the reference drawing, with lengths and angles entered as dimensions.

## What could be improved?

Next time I would plan which constraints to use before drawing, so the sketch becomes fully defined with fewer dimensions and is easier to edit.
