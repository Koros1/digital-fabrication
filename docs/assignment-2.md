# Assignment 2 — Rocket Design in Tinkercad

## What was the goal?
The goal was to explore basic 3D modelling in Tinkercad by creating two different rocket designs and comparing their shapes, proportions, and visual details.

## What did I do?
I created two rocket models. The first has a tall, narrow pink body, a light blue nose, and dark blue fins. The second has a shorter, rounded white body, a red nose, three orange circular windows, and dark blue fins. The first design explores a simple, elongated rocket shape, while the second takes a more playful approach.

## What tools and materials did I use?
I used Tinkercad’s browser-based 3D design workspace and geometric shapes to build the models. The project required a computer and an internet connection; no physical materials were used at this stage.

## What could be improved?
The main areas to check are the alignment of the nose and body, the spacing of the fins, and whether all parts connect properly. These details affect the appearance of the models and would need closer inspection before 3D printing.

## How would I improve the designs?
I would inspect each model from different angles, adjust the position and size of any misaligned parts, and check the connections between the components.

## What was the result?
I completed two visually distinct rocket models in the same Tinkercad workspace. This project helped me explore how changes in proportions, colours, and details can create different designs based on the same theme.
